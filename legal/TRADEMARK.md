# NAEOS Trademark & Brand Policy

Status: Active draft  
Version: 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Apache-2.0 licenses NAEOS software; it does not grant trademark rights. This policy separates software licensing from brand usage.

## 2. Project marks

The repository currently identifies the following project marks:

- NAEOS
- NEIR
- NAEOS logos and associated visual identity

The existence of a project mark in this list is not a statement that a registration exists in any jurisdiction.

## 3. Permitted descriptive use

Third parties may make truthful, non-misleading references to NAEOS for purposes such as:

- describing compatibility;
- identifying unmodified NAEOS software;
- linking to the project;
- discussing integrations or technical evaluation.

Such references must not imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or official status unless authorized.

## 4. Restricted use

Do not use NAEOS marks in a manner that:

- implies project endorsement without authorization;
- falsely suggests official certification;
- creates a confusingly similar product identity;
- incorporates NAEOS branding into a competing product name without permission;
- removes or alters required attribution in a misleading way.

## 5. Registration workstream

Before filing or claiming trademark registration, perform jurisdiction-specific clearance for:

- word marks;
- relevant logos;
- relevant product/service classes;
- existing conflicting marks.

Maintain filing numbers, jurisdictions, owners, classes, renewal dates, and counsel records in the private legal register.

## 6. Relationship to Apache-2.0

The Apache License remains the software license. This policy does not modify the software license.

The repository `NOTICE` remains the public attribution/branding notice.
