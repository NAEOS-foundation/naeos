# NAEOS Legal Architecture

Status: Active draft  
Version: 1.0  
Owner: NAEOS Foundation / NAEOS OSS project maintainers

This directory defines the repository-level legal and governance controls for NAEOS. It complements, and does not replace, applicable law, executed agreements, or professional legal advice.

## Scope

The legal architecture covers:

- intellectual-property provenance and ownership evidence;
- open-source licensing and contributor rights;
- trademark and branding boundaries;
- third-party dependency and attribution obligations;
- AI-assisted contribution provenance;
- release-level legal verification;
- future commercial-service boundaries.

## Current licensing baseline

The NAEOS repository is licensed under Apache License 2.0. Contributions are accepted through the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO), as documented in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

This architecture does not relicense existing NAEOS code.

## Authority

Repository license terms are authoritative through `LICENSE`. Attribution and branding requirements are documented in `NOTICE`. Contributor submission requirements are documented in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the DCO workflow.

Where these documents conflict with an executed agreement or applicable law, the applicable agreement or law controls.

## Open legal questions

The following require confirmation by the appropriate rights holder or qualified counsel before commercial launch:

1. The exact legal entity and jurisdiction represented by "NAEOS Foundation".
2. Trademark availability, ownership, and filing strategy for NAEOS, NEIR, and associated logos.
3. Ownership and assignment records for pre-existing first-party intellectual property.
4. Terms for hosted services, enterprise offerings, support, SLAs, and data processing.
5. Jurisdiction-specific privacy and data-protection obligations.
6. Any patent strategy or defensive publication policy.

No unresolved item should be represented publicly as legally settled.
