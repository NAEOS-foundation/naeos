This Cookie Policy explains how the NAEOS website (naeos.dev) uses cookies and
similar technologies.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help
websites remember your preferences and understand how the site is used.

## 2. Cookies We Use

NAEOS uses **no tracking or advertising cookies**. The only local storage used
is a flag recording your cookie consent choice — this is not a cookie and
contains no personal data.

### Third-party services that may set cookies

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| GitHub | May set cookies when you visit GitHub-powered pages (stats, repository links). Governed by [GitHub's cookie policy](https://github.com/privacy) |
| Google Fonts | Serves fonts; may set cookies as described in [Google's cookie policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) |
| Netlify | Hosting; may set functional cookies as described in [Netlify's cookie policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) |

## 3. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies in your browser settings. Declining the
cookie consent banner does not affect the core functionality of the website —
the site works without cookies.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on
this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
