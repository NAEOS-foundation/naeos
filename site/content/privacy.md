_Last updated: August 16, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information the NAEOS project collects,
how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers the NAEOS CLI tool, the
website (naeos.dev), and the NAEOS Discord community.

## 1. What We Collect

### NAEOS CLI Tool

NAEOS is a command-line tool that runs locally on your machine. **We do not
collect or transmit any data from your usage of the CLI tool.** No telemetry,
no crash reporting, no analytics. Your specifications, projects, and generated
code never leave your machine.

### Website (naeos.dev)

When you visit the website, we may collect:

- **Anonymous usage data** via privacy-focused analytics (no personal data)
- **Newsletter email address** when you voluntarily subscribe — stored with
  your language preference and subscription source so we can send NAEOS updates
- **GitHub API data** — when you visit pages that display GitHub stats (stars,
  forks), your browser fetches data from the public GitHub API. No authentication
  data is collected
- **Cookie consent preference** — stored locally in your browser (see the
  [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/))

### Discord Community

When you join the NAEOS Discord server, Discord processes your account
information (username, avatar, roles, messages) under
[Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). The NAEOS bot only
reads channel messages needed to post announcements and run its commands.

## 2. What We Do NOT Collect

- NAEOS does not collect telemetry from the CLI tool
- NAEOS does not track your specifications, projects, or generated code
- NAEOS does not use cookies for tracking purposes (only a local storage flag
  for cookie consent)
- NAEOS does not sell or rent your personal data to anyone

| Service | Purpose | Policy |

## 4. How We Use Your Data

Your email address (if subscribed) is used solely to send NAEOS updates,
releases, and launch announcements. We do not share your email with third
parties for marketing.

## 5. Data Retention

- Newsletter email: retained until you unsubscribe or request removal
- Analytics: aggregated, anonymized, and retained without personal identifiers

## 6. Your Choices

- **Newsletter**: unsubscribe via the link in any email, or contact us
- **Cookie consent**: decline via the banner — the site works without consent
- **Access/erasure**: request removal of your data by contacting us (Section 8)

## 7. Data Protection (GDPR/CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have rights to
access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. Contact us to exercise
these rights. We process data based on your consent and legitimate interests
(improving the project and communicating updates).

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue on the
[NAEOS GitHub repository](https://github.com/NAEOS-foundation/naeos) or contact
the maintainer at [bayu@naeos.dev](mailto:bayu@naeos.dev).
