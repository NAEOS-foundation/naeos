## 1. Welcome message — `#welcome`

> # Welcome to the NAEOS Community
>
> **NAEOS** is a declarative engineering platform: describe your system once,
> and it builds, validates, and evolves real software — for humans and AI.
>
> **Specify once. Build anywhere.**
>
> ## Start here
> 1. Read the rules in `#rules`.
> 2. Say hi in `#introductions`.
> 3. Try NAEOS in 30 seconds:
>    ```bash
>    curl -fsSL https://naeos.dev/install.sh | sh
>    naeos create my-app
>    naeos run --config config.yaml --input-file spec.yaml
>    ```
> 4. Get help in `#help-requests`, share what you build in `#showcase`.
>
> ## Useful links
> - Docs: https://docs.naeos.dev
> - GitHub: https://github.com/NAEOS-foundation/naeos
> - Website: https://naeos.dev
> - Whitepaper: https://naeos.dev/whitepaper
>
> We're glad you're here. Ask anything — the community answers fast.

## 2. Rules — `#rules`

> # Community Rules
>
> 1. **Be excellent to each other.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Personal
>    attacks, harassment, and hate speech are not tolerated.
> 2. **Stay on topic.** Engineering discussions belong in the engineering channels;
>    casual chat goes in `#off-topic`.
> 3. **Search before asking.** Check `#faq` and the docs first — then ask in
>    `#help-requests`. Include your spec/CLI output when relevant.
> 4. **No spam.** Self-promotion belongs in `#showcase` and must add value.
>    No vote-brigading or paid promotion, ever.
> 5. **Respect the license.** NAEOS is Apache 2.0 — keep attribution where required.
> 6. **Follow Discord's Terms of Service.** No illegal content, no pirated material.
> 7. **Moderators have the final word.** Warnings escalate: warn → mute → kick → ban.
>
> Report issues to any `@Moderator` or open a ticket in `#mod-chat`.

## 3. Community guidelines — `#welcome` (or server guide)

> ## How this community works
> - **Open source first.** Everything we build is public. RFCs, ADRs, and roadmap
>   discussions live on GitHub — Discord is where the conversation happens.
> - **Help generously.** Someone asked something you know? Answer it. One good
>   answer helps hundreds of lurkers.
> - **Constructive disagreement.** Found a design decision you disagree with?
>   Say why, with evidence. That's how the spec evolves.
> - **AI-assisted is welcome.** We build AI tooling ourselves; using it here is
>   normal. Just be honest about what's human and what's not.
