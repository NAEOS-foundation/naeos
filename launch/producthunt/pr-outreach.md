# PR & Media Outreach — Dev Tool Launch

Channels that accept public submissions or pitches for developer-tool launches.
Prioritized by effort vs. payoff for an open-source dev tool (NAEOS, PH 18 Aug).

---

## Tier 1 — High payoff, zero cost, submit yourself

| Channel | How | URL / Contact | Timeline |
|---------|-----|---------------|----------|
| **Product Hunt** | Main launch (already planned) | producthunt.com | 18 Aug 00:01 PT |
| **Hacker News (Show HN)** | Self-post; content ready in `social-posts.md` | news.ycombinator.com | Launch day, 12:00–14:00 PT |
| **HackerNoon "Proof of Usefulness"** | Submit project → auto-generated draft → publish | proofofusefulness.com | Now (publish within days) |
| **DEV Community (dev.to)** | Sign in → Create Post; write real article + backlink | dev.to | Week of launch |
| **Reddit** (r/devops, r/golang, r/programming) | Self-post; content ready in `social-posts.md`; check each sub's self-promo rules | reddit.com | Launch day |
| **Indie Hackers** | Post launch + learnings | indiehackers.com | Launch day |
| **HashiCorp/industry blogs** | Guest post / comment (long-shot) | — | Post-launch |

## Tier 2 — Newsletter partners (1k–20k subscribers, best effort-to-payoff)

Find + pitch newsletters via **Substack / Kit / Beehiiv** search:

- Search terms: `"developer tools" newsletter`, `"open source" newsletter`,
  `"spec driven"`, `"indie hackers" digest`, `"Go" newsletter weekly`
- Pitch format (3 short paragraphs): story angle → why their audience cares
  (reference their recent issue) → low-friction ask (free account to try / guest
  post outline)
- Do NOT attach media kits. No attachments.

## Tier 3 — Major tech press (long-shot; skip unless traction appears)

TechCrunch / The Verge / Ars Technica / VentureBeat / Wired:

- Requires real traction proof (users, GitHub stars, revenue) — no "we launched"
  without it
- **Embargo pitch 2–3 weeks before** launch (i.e. late July/early Aug for 18 Aug)
- Contributor op-ed path: TechCrunch accepts pitches to `tips@techcrunch.com`
  (short idea + headline; not bulk press releases)
- Read the beat writer's recent articles; pitch personally, not via press release

---

## Recommended plan (for a 18 Aug launch)

1. **Now–17 Aug:** Submit HackerNoon PoU (auto-publish); write dev.to article;
   subscribe to 5 dev-tool newsletters and study their format.
2. **D-3 (15 Aug):** Pitch 5–8 newsletters with the 3-paragraph format.
3. **D-day (18 Aug):** PH post → Show HN → Reddit → Indie Hackers → dev.to.
4. **D+1:** Follow up with newsletters that showed interest; share PH results.

## What NOT to do

- No bulk email of personal addresses (spam; PH bans paid votes; reporters ignore
  mass press releases)
- No paid upvotes / reviews
- Don't pitch major press the week of launch — too late, and no traction hook
