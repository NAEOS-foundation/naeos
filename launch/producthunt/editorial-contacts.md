# Editorial & Media Contacts — Dev Tool Launch

Public editorial inboxes and submission channels (published by the outlets for
receiving pitches). For an open-source dev tool like NAEOS, target the
dev-tool/enterprise beat writers listed below. **Do not** email personal
addresses you can't verify are public; prefer these official channels.

---

## Pitch guidelines (from research)

- **Timing:** embargo pitch 2–3 weeks before launch; major-press outreach the
  week of launch is too late.
- **Subject line formula:** "`[Product] launches [date] — [one-line value prop]`"
  or "`Launching on PH: [specific metric/claim]`".
- **Body (under 150 words):** news in one sentence → why it matters → key
  traction metric → your credential → the offer (exclusive/embargo/demo).
- **No attachments.** Paste the announcement in the email body.
- **Follow up:** 24–48h post-launch with results ("We hit #1 on PH this morning").
- **Realistic bar:** Tier 1–2 major outlets need real traction proof (users,
  stars, revenue). With ~6 GitHub stars at launch, lead with the technical
  novelty + open-source angle, and lean on Tier 3 + Tier 2 of `pr-outreach.md`.
