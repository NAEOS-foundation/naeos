# Academic Partner Brief

## 1. Partner objective

Study a bounded NAEOS engineering question through research, benchmarking, architecture review, or student contribution.

## 2. Current NAEOS capability relevant to the partnership

The repository provides NEIR, validation, generation, policy, provenance, benchmark, fuzz-testing, and testing documentation that can support a reproducible study.

## 3. Proposed pilot

Choose one research question, dataset or synthetic fixture, method, and evaluation window.

## 4. Partner contribution

Potential contribution: researcher or student time, benchmark design, analysis, architecture review, or academic dissemination, subject to institutional approval.

## 5. NAEOS contribution

NAEOS can provide an Apache-2.0 codebase, public specifications, test environment guidance, maintainer feedback, and contributor recognition under project rules.

## 6. Expected technical output

Reproducible benchmark or study artifacts, methodology, results, limitations, and proposed follow-up.

## 7. Success criteria

The study states its method, controls, repository version, inputs, outputs, and limitations so another researcher can inspect the result.

## 8. Timeline

Target two to four weeks for an initial study plan or benchmark iteration.

## 9. No-cash collaboration model

No stipend, grant, or institutional funding is assumed. Participation is subject to the institution's policies.

## 10. Next step

Agree on research question, supervision, contribution records, data handling, publication review, and licensing.
