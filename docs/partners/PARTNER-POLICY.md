# NAEOS Partner Contribution Policy

1. NAEOS remains open-source under the repository's Apache License 2.0, subject to the license and applicable notices.
2. No partner receives ownership of the project merely by contributing.
3. Contributions must respect Apache 2.0, repository licensing, and [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), including the DCO.
4. No exclusivity is created unless separately reviewed and explicitly approved in writing.
5. Partner names, logos, and branding must be used only with permission and according to applicable brand guidance.
6. Public case studies, quotes, metrics, and partner statements require partner approval.
7. Technical claims must be verifiable against repository evidence or clearly identified as proposed work.
8. NAEOS must not imply partner endorsement, certification, or affiliation without permission.
9. Security-sensitive integrations require appropriate threat, access, data-handling, and review controls.
10. Contributions must comply with repository contribution, conduct, security, and licensing rules.

Do not put credentials, API keys, private infrastructure details, confidential partner information, or personal data in Git. Use the environment-variable and secret-management mechanisms already used by the repository, and report security issues through [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
