# Academic Pilot

## Pilot Objective
Produce a reproducible benchmark, architecture review, or research note about a defined NAEOS workflow.

## Problem
State the research question, hypothesis, or engineering uncertainty.

## Proposed Integration
Use public repository code, specifications, tests, and benchmark tooling with a defined version and fixture.

## Partner Contribution
Potential student or researcher time, study design, analysis, or review, subject to institutional approval.

## NAEOS Contribution
Public Apache-2.0 environment, maintainer coordination, technical context, and contributor recognition under project rules.

## Deliverables
Method, fixture, benchmark or review output, results, limitations, and reproducibility instructions.

## Technical Acceptance Criteria
The method and inputs are inspectable, results are repeatable within stated limits, and unsupported claims are excluded.

## Timeline
Two to four weeks for the initial study iteration.

## Publicity / Case Study
Publication or case study requires agreement on attribution, data, review, and licensing.

## Exit Criteria
The study record is complete and the parties decide whether to publish, revise, or stop.
