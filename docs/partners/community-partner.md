# Community Partner Brief

## 1. Partner objective

Help developers understand, test, or contribute to a specific NAEOS workflow.

## 2. Current NAEOS capability relevant to the partnership

Relevant material may include the CLI pipeline, specification language, NEIR, plugins, profiles, AI integrations, documentation, or examples. Select one concrete topic.

## 3. Proposed pilot

Run one technical presentation, workshop, newsletter feature, office hour, or contributor session.

## 4. Partner contribution

Potential contribution: event space, community distribution, feedback, speakers, or contributor recruitment, subject to the community's approval.

## 5. NAEOS contribution

NAEOS can provide technical speakers or content, workshop material, example repositories, and maintainer follow-up.

## 6. Expected technical output

One published or delivered technical session plus feedback and any reproducible examples created during it.

## 7. Success criteria

The session has a defined audience, accurate claims, accessible materials, and a documented feedback path.

## 8. Timeline

Target one event or content cycle over two to four weeks.

## 9. No-cash collaboration model

No sponsorship fee, audience size, or promotion is guaranteed. Both parties approve the format and claims.

## 10. Next step

Confirm audience, topic, date, format, speaker, materials, moderation, and recording permissions.
