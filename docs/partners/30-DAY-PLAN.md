# NAEOS Partner Program: 30-Day Execution Plan

**Primary target:** three technically scoped pilot partnerships. The target is not money raised, credits obtained, or partner prestige.

## Week 1: Prepare

- Finalize partner documentation and policy.
- Create a partner landing page or documentation entry point.
- Define three pilot proposals from [PARTNER-REQUESTS.md](../../PARTNER-REQUESTS.md).
- Use the [recommended first integration pilot](pilots/first-integration-pilot.md) as the partner-neutral starting brief.
- Prepare outreach, architecture, security, and acceptance-criteria assets.

## Week 2: Reach out

- Contact technology prospects with a concrete integration brief.
- Contact cloud prospects with a bounded sandbox proposal.
- Contact academic prospects with a research or benchmark question.
- Contact developer communities with a workshop proposal.

## Week 3: Qualify

- Hold technical conversations.
- Record partner fit using [PARTNER-FIT.md](PARTNER-FIT.md).
- Confirm access, eligibility, security, owners, and publicity constraints.
- Select pilot opportunities with mutually agreed scope.

## Week 4: Execute

- Launch the first pilot or pilots.
- Produce the integration, test, benchmark, or workshop output.
- Document the result and unresolved limitations.
- Publish a case study only after partner approval.

## Operating rules

Use the partner policy, repository contribution rules, DCO, Code of Conduct, and SECURITY.md throughout. A delayed or declined proposal is a valid outcome; do not represent interest as a partnership.
