# Pilot Partner Brief

## 1. Partner objective

Validate one NAEOS workflow against a real but authorized engineering use case.

## 2. Current NAEOS capability relevant to the partnership

Use only a verified capability such as specification parsing, NEIR construction, validation, generation, policy evaluation, AI context, plugin execution, or cloud planning.

## 3. Proposed pilot

Select a non-sensitive workload or isolated integration environment with a clear baseline and exit condition.

## 4. Partner contribution

Potential contribution: test users, workload description, integration environment, technical contact, and feedback, subject to authorization.

## 5. NAEOS contribution

NAEOS can provide setup guidance, engineering support, documentation, issue triage, and accepted open-source improvements.

## 6. Expected technical output

A reproducible pilot record, integration notes, acceptance results, and a list of unresolved limitations.

## 7. Success criteria

The agreed workflow completes on the agreed fixture, produces inspectable output, and meets the written acceptance criteria.

## 8. Timeline

Target two to four weeks, with a short review at the end of each week.

## 9. No-cash collaboration model

No paid deployment, production commitment, or commercial outcome is assumed.

## 10. Next step

Confirm workload, data boundary, access, owners, acceptance criteria, and whether any result may be published.
