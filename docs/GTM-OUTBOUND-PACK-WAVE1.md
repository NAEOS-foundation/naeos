# GTM Outbound Pack — Wave 1

Research date: 2026-09-25

## Purpose

Convert the Wave 1 account and contact intelligence into a controlled outbound execution pack.

NAEOS positioning:

> NAEOS is the control plane for AI-native software engineering.

Core message:

> AI agents propose changes. NAEOS applies engineering policy and authorization before execution, then records evidence for verification.

This pack contains message variants and a tracking contract. It does not imply that any listed person or company has expressed interest, evaluated NAEOS, or agreed to receive outreach.

## Execution rules

1. Use only a verified public role/persona as the research target.
2. Re-open the cited source immediately before sending and confirm the role and trigger are still current.
3. Personalize around a documented engineering signal, not an assumed buying need.
4. Do not claim compatibility, partnership, customer status, evaluation, approval, or purchasing intent without direct evidence.
5. Do not infer private email addresses or other private contact data.
6. Keep the first touch problem-led and low-pressure.
7. The technical follow-up should introduce the control-plane boundary: proposal → policy decision → authorized execution → observation/evidence.
8. The pilot CTA must remain bounded: 30–45 days, one team, 1–3 repos, one AI-assisted workflow.
9. Record every touch in the tracking schema below.
10. Stop or revise the sequence when the recipient asks not to be contacted.

> The model we’re testing is intentionally narrow:
>
> **Model proposes → Policy decides → Runtime executes → Observation records.**
>
> The important boundary is authorization, not prompt quality. We want the system to prove what was requested, what policy allowed, what actually executed, and what verification observed.
>
> If that maps to a real workflow on your side, I can show the architecture and a bounded evaluation path.

## Pipeline state contract

Allowed stages:

Target → Researched → Contacted → Engaged → Discovery → Technical Evaluation → Design Partner → Pilot → Evidence Review → Commercial → Won → Expansion

Do not advance a stage merely because an outreach message was sent. Advance only when the corresponding observable event occurs.

## Governance boundary

Outbound execution itself should remain evidence-based and reversible:

- Research is a proposal/input, not proof of recipient interest.
- A personalized message is not proof of a buying signal.
- A reply is not proof of a pilot.
- A scheduled technical evaluation is not proof of commercial intent.
- A pilot is not proof of production adoption.
- Record source, timestamp, decision, and outcome for each material transition.

## Immediate execution checklist

- [ ] Revalidate role and trigger immediately before sending.
