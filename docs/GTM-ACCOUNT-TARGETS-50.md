## Account research fields

For every account capture:
- company
- country/region
- engineering scale estimate
- GitHub usage evidence
- AI-agent adoption evidence
- platform engineering leader
- engineering leader
- security leader
- developer productivity leader
- likely workflow
- likely pain
- public trigger/event
- personalization hook
- contact status
- pipeline stage
- next action
- source URL
- evidence date

Do not infer AI-agent adoption merely from company size. Record public evidence or validate it during discovery.
