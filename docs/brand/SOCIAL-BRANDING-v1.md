# NAEOS Social Branding v1.0

## Canonical identity
- Master mark: NAEOS infinity-N
- Background: Deep Navy `#0B1220`
- Primary accents: Electric Blue `#3B82F6`, Cyan `#22D3EE`, Violet `#8B5CF6`
- Text: Off White `#F8FAFC`
- Secondary text: Slate `#94A3B8`

## Avatar
Use the symbol only. Keep the mark centered on a square Deep Navy field. Do not add the wordmark at small sizes.

## Social card
Use the dynamic website Open Graph image as the canonical technical/social preview. Keep messaging concise and evidence-led.

## Content hierarchy
1. NAEOS infinity-N
2. Technical statement or article title
3. Short supporting context
4. `naeos.dev`

## Voice
Precise, engineering-led, evidence-led. Avoid hype, generic AI imagery, and unsubstantiated claims.

## Approved positioning
NAEOS adds governance, verification, auditability, and controlled runtime boundaries around AI coding agents.

## Channel application
- LinkedIn: technical insight, founder journal, engineering notes
- GitHub: project updates, architecture, release notes
- Slack/Discord: community updates and contributor guidance
- X/community: concise technical observations and release signals

## Do not
- use robot/brain/circuit-board clichés
- recolor the infinity-N outside the approved system
- stretch, skew, rotate, or add effects to the mark
- use competing standalone ecosystem logos
